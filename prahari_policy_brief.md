## 2. Priority Statutory Interventions

### 1. Prioritize Unannounced Labour Inspections in High-Thermal Districts (CRITICAL_HIGH)
- **Domain:** MOHRE Statutory Midday Work Ban (Ministerial Resolution No. 44)
- **Target Districts:** Al Quoz Industrial, Al Quoz Industrial
- **Evidence Justification:** Surpassed 182.5 cumulative hours above WBGT 32°C and recorded 26 days of critical midday thermal stress. Mandates active verification of shaded rest shelters and chilled potable electrolyte stations.
- **Expected Protection:** Enforcement covers an estimated 40,000 outdoor industrial workers across monitored sectors.

### 2. Stage Municipal Shaded Hydration Corridors and Misting Hubs (ELEVATED)
- **Domain:** Dubai Municipality Occupational Health Infrastructure
- **Target Districts:** Al Quoz Industrial, Jebel Ali Port & Industrial
- **Evidence Justification:** Industrial heavy machinery clusters exhibit combined radiant heat amplification. Pre-staging mobile cooling hubs within 200m walking radius reduces hyperthermia triage load.
- **Expected Protection:** Projected to avert up to 65% of ambulatory heat exhaustion progressions when paired with 3.4-day lead times.

### 3. Mandate Water Mist Dust Suppression and FFP2 Respirators for Outdoor Handlers (MODERATE_PRECAUTIONARY)
- **Domain:** Federal Authority for Nuclear Regulation / Air Quality Standards
- **Target Districts:** Al Quoz Industrial
- **Evidence Justification:** District registered 17 days exceeding WHO PM2.5 limits (35 µg/m³). Concomitant heat and particulate exposure accelerates cardiopulmonary strain.
- **Expected Protection:** Mitigates compound acute respiratory syndromic presentations by an estimated 40%.
