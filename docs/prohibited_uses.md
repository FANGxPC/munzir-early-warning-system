# PRAHARI Prohibited Uses Statement

To protect civil liberties, employee rights, and medical confidentiality, PRAHARI incorporates policy and architectural prohibitions.

## Strictly Prohibited Uses

1. **Individual Worker Tracking & Disciplinary Actions:**
   - PRAHARI data must never be used to evaluate individual employee attendance, productivity, sick leave authenticity, or employment standing.
   - Any attempt to query or infer individual identities from workplace rates is prohibited by system design (small-cell suppression and minimum cohort sizes).

2. **Insurance Underwriting & Discriminatory Premium Adjustments:**
   - Aggregate early warning indicators must not be utilized by commercial insurers to deny coverage, adjust individual premiums, or penalize specific workforces.

3. **Autonomous Punitive Measures:**
   - No automated sanction, fine, or shutdown may be executed purely by model inference. PRAHARI outputs are advisory decision-support mechanisms requiring human-in-the-loop verification.

4. **Public Stigmatization:**
   - Granular spatial cluster scans are restricted to authorized public health and occupational officers. Public views are aggregated to district/state boundaries with differential privacy protections to prevent neighborhood or facility stigmatization.
