Methodological Limitations & Truth in Science Disclosures
  • Wet-Bulb Natural Wick Approximation: On-site reference WBGT uses Stull's (2011) psychrometric equation. It carries ±0.5°C to ±1.0°C screening uncertainty relative to an actively aspirated natural wet-bulb.
  • Sensor Placement: DHT22 air/humidity sensor is shielded from direct solar radiation in a louvered enclosure; DS18B20 black globe is exposed to ambient radiant flux. Local micro-shading creates legitimate site-specific offsets.
  • Data Duration: Live Bronze store holds 3 days of telemetry. The 30-day paired campaign benchmark was evaluated over authentic Open-Meteo ERA5 hourly meteorology for Dubai.
  • Spatial Transfer Boundary: Unsensored sites cannot claim empirical before/after error reduction and are strictly flagged UNVALIDATED-SPATIALLY with conformal prediction intervals expanded by +50%.
