⚠️ Methodological Truth in Science & Operational Disclosures
  • Wet-Bulb Approximation: On-site reference WBGT uses Stull's (2011) psychrometric equation rather than an aspirated wet wick, carrying ±0.5°C to ±1.0°C screening uncertainty.
  • Sensor Placement: DHT22 shielded inside louvered Stevenson screen; DS18B20 black globe exposed to radiant heat. Microclimate offsets reflect real asphalt/steel thermal absorption.
  • Duration & Campaign: Live Bronze store holds 3 days of telemetry. A 30-day paired campaign benchmark was evaluated over authentic Open-Meteo ERA5 hourly meteorology.
  • Spatial Transfer Boundary: Unsensored sites are strictly flagged UNVALIDATED-SPATIALLY with conformal prediction intervals widened by +50%. No empirical before/after error is claimed on unsensored locations.
