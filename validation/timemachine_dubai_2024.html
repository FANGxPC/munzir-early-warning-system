Rigorous Data Provenance & Methodological Limitations

1. Authentic Weather Source: Open-Meteo Archived Forecast API (historical-forecast-api.open-meteo.com). Uses archived numerical weather prediction model runs (ECMWF IFS / NOAA GFS) produced at that time, rather than post-hoc assimilated reanalysis.
2. Authentic Clinical Outcome Ground Truth: U.S. Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) National Syndromic Surveillance Program (NSSP), published in MMWR Vol. 70, No. 29 (Schramm et al., 2021; DOI: 10.15585/mmwr.mm7029e1). Represents real emergency department visits across HHS Region 10.
3. Simulated Reporting Delay Specification: Facility transmission latency is simulated using an empirical 3-day geometric distribution: 70% reported on D+1, 20% on D+2, 10% on D+3 (mean lag: 1.4 days), adhering to CDC NSSP facility latency profiles.
4. Leakage Control: Baseline parameters and thresholds fitted strictly on data prior to 2021-06-24. Zero parameter tuning performed on the heat dome window.
