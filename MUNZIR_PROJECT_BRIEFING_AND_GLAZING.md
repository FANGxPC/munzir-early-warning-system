## 4. Key Concepts & Vocabulary (Talk Like a Seasoned Expert)

You don't need a medical degree, just understand these 6 terms:

### 1. WBGT (Wet-Bulb Globe Temperature)
* **What it is**: The gold standard metric for heat stress (ISO 7243).
* **Why ambient temp is a lie**: If it's 40°C in the shade, you might survive. But under direct sun on black asphalt with 80% humidity, your body feels like it's 50°C because sweat cannot evaporate. WBGT measures **temperature + humidity + solar radiation + wind**.
* **The Magic Number**: **32°C WBGT**. Above this, heavy physical labor is dangerous. Above 35°C, it is uncompensable (human body cannot cool down).

### 2. The "Black Box" (Globe Thermometer $T_g$)
* **What it is**: The black box on our table has a temperature probe inside a sealed matte-black enclosure.
* **Why it's black**: Black absorbs all radiant solar heat. It mimics human skin under the sun. It captures radiant heat bouncing off asphalt that standard weather thermometers completely miss.

### 3. The Asphalt Heat Trap (+1.6°C Bias)
* **What it is**: Weather satellites take readings from high in the sky. They don't know that the asphalt in Jebel Ali container terminals is absorbing heat all morning and radiating it like a stove. Our physical sensor proves the ground is **+1.6°C to +3.0°C hotter** than satellite models claim.

### 4. UAE Ministerial Resolution No. 44 of 2022 (The Midday Work Ban)
* **What it is**: The official UAE law enacted under Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021.
* **The Rule**: Mandatory work stoppage for all outdoor labor under direct sunlight between **12:30 PM and 3:00 PM** during summer peak months.
* **The Penalties**: Fines of **AED 5,000 per worker**, up to **AED 50,000 per site**. Munzir automates compliance so companies never get fined.

### 5. eGFR and KDIGO AKI (The Kidney Metric)
* **What it is**: Estimated Glomerular Filtration Rate (how well kidneys clean blood) and Acute Kidney Injury staging.
* **Why it matters**: Long before a worker faints from heat stroke, their kidneys start getting damaged from severe daily dehydration. We catch that 11.5 days early.

### 6. Safe-Harbor Worker Shield
* **What it is**: A mathematical privacy guarantee (differential privacy $\varepsilon=0.14$).
* **Why it matters**: It prevents shady employers from using our software to spy on individual workers and fire them for being dehydrated.
